================================================================================
SECTION 5: P2P PAYMENT APP FRAUD (Venmo, PayPal, Square Cash, etc.)
================================================================================

Increasing risk: Scammers trick victims into sending money via P2P apps, which are 
harder to reverse than bank transfers.

5.1 IMMEDIATE REPORTING (P2P Apps)
───────────────────────────────────

P2P apps have limited fraud protection compared to banks.

Steps:
1. Report to the P2P app immediately:
   • Go to app settings → Report a Problem
   • Or contact app's fraud department
   • Provide transaction ID, recipient username, amount, date
2. Report to your bank (if linked debit/credit card):
   • Bank may reverse through underlying payment method
3. Report to FBI IC3: www.ic3.gov
4. File police report (local non-emergency)

Likelihood of Recovery:
  • 20-30% recovery rate (much lower than wire transfers)
  • P2P apps freeze suspect accounts but don't guarantee return
  • If recipient already withdrew cash, recovery unlikely

EFTA Protection on P2P:
  • P2P transfers initiated through linked debit accounts may have SOME EFTA 
    protection (recent CFPB guidance)
  • Credit card P2P: May fall under Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) instead
  • Terms vary by app; check app's terms

5.2 DISPUTE PROCEDURES (App-Specific)
──────────────────────────────────────

Venmo:
  • Dispute link: Settings → About Venmo → Contact Us → Report an Issue
  • Success rate: Low if recipient already transferred out
  • Venmo holds disputed funds but doesn't guarantee return
  • Must report within reasonable time (check Venmo terms)

PayPal:
  • File dispute through Resolution Center
  • May offer buyer protection if linked to PayPal Wallet
  • But protection limited for person-to-person transfers
  • Different rules for Goods/Services vs Friends & Family payments
  • Friends & Family transfers have almost zero protection

Square Cash (Cash App):
  • Report to Square through app support
  • Very limited chargeback protections
  • Recommend contacting linked bank instead
  • Cash App prioritizes quick transactions over fraud protection

Google Pay & Apple Pay:
  • P2P transfers through Google Pay/Apple Pay have variable protection
  • Depends on underlying payment method (debit vs credit card)
  • Report to payment processor and your bank

Recommendation:
For all P2P fraud, contact underlying bank first (if debit card linked) as bank 
has better fraud reversal power than P2P app itself.

5.3 DETAILED RECOVERY STEPS
─────────────────────────────

Step 1: Contact P2P App (First 24 Hours)
  • Note exact transaction ID (found in transaction history)
  • Document recipient username/account name
  • Note if recipient account is still active
  • Take screenshots of transaction

Step 2: Contact Your Bank (First 24-48 Hours)
  • If debit card linked: Bank may reverse transaction as unauthorized debit
  • Provide P2P app transaction ID and bank transaction details
  • Request investigation under EFTA (if debit-linked)
  • Request chargeback under FCBA (if credit card-linked)

Step 3: Freeze Recipient Account (If Possible)
  • P2P app fraud team can flag recipient account
  • May prevent recipient from withdrawing funds pending investigation
  • Success depends on speed of reporting

Step 4: FBI & Police Reporting
  • File IC3 complaint: www.ic3.gov
  • File local police report (non-emergency)
  • Attach bank documentation

5.4 LEGAL PROTECTIONS (Limited)
─────────────────────────────────

EFTA Protection (Debit Card Linked):
  • May apply if debit card linked to P2P app
  • Liability: $50 (if reported within 2 days), $500 (within 60 days)
  • Bank must investigate
  • Check specific app's terms

FCBA Protection (Credit Card Linked):
  • Fair Credit Billing Act may protect credit card P2P transfers
  • Dispute period: 60 days from charge appearing on statement
  • Issuer must investigate
  • Varies by credit card company

Terms & Conditions Loopholes:
  • Many P2P apps state "Person-to-Person transfers are final"
  • Terms may exempt fraud protection for P2P
  • BUT EFTA/FCBA may override unfavorable terms
  • Consult attorney if app denies claim

Legal References:
  - 15 U.S.C. § 1693 (EFTA - applies to debit)
  - 15 U.S.C. § 1666 (FCBA - applies to credit cards)

================================================================================
APP COMPARISON - FRAUD RECOVERY
================================================================================

Venmo:        Low recovery (no EFTA), account freeze possible
PayPal:       Medium recovery (Friends & Family=none, Goods/Services=higher)
Cash App:     Very low recovery, limited protections
Google Pay:   Depends on underlying payment method
Apple Pay:    Depends on underlying payment method

BEST STRATEGY: Use debit card through app (better EFTA protection) rather than 
P2P-specific "Friends & Family" transfers which have near-zero protection.
